One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened
today — something that would make a reader smile. Entry for the make.fun
bounty [Make the Judge Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes a single line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is fixed and deterministic: every run
produces byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## The line

> At 6:40 the lift stopped on the wrong floor, and a neighbour I'd
> never met pressed her umbrella into my hand and walked off into the
> rain without one — I still don't know her name, only her laugh.

Judged on subject first, then warmth, then surprise, then one concrete
detail, then fresh wording. The line aims at all five: a small kindness
this morning, a stranger's umbrella, the rain coming in sideways, and a
laugh instead of a name.
